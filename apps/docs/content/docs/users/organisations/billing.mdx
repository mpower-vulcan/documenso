## One plan per organisation

Each organisation on SignCoach has its own plan. The plan covers every team and member inside that
organisation. Creating a second organisation starts a second, separate plan.

<Callout type="info">
  Plans, included AI credits and self-serve billing are being finalised. Until then, plans are set up for
  you by MPower Vulcan. Write to [sign@mpowerglobal.ai](mailto:sign@mpowerglobal.ai) to change a plan.
</Callout>

## AI credits

Features that run SignCoach's AI, such as the plain-English explanation shown to signers, draw on a
credit allowance that belongs to the organisation. One document explained is counted once no matter
how many people sign it. Organisations can also connect their own AI provider key instead of using
credits. Details and limits will be documented here when the coach is live.
