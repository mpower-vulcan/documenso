SignCoach is an e-signature service by MPower Vulcan LLC (Las Vegas, Nevada). You upload a document,
place the fields a signer must complete, send it, and get back a signed PDF with a tamper-evident audit
trail and a completion certificate.

## What is different about SignCoach

- **Plain-English explanations for signers.** SignCoach is built to explain what a document says before
  someone signs it: what it is, who the parties are, what you are agreeing to, the money and dates, and
  anything worth reading twice. This coach panel is rolling out and will be documented here when it is
  live. It explains; it is not a lawyer and it does not give legal advice.
- **Works with the tools you already use.** Anything a sender can do in the app can also be done through
  the API (see the Developers section), and SignCoach connectors for AI assistants are planned.

## Signing in and accounts

New accounts are created by invitation. If you were invited, follow the link in your email. If you need an
account, contact the organisation that sent you here or write to [sign@mpowerglobal.ai](mailto:sign@mpowerglobal.ai).
