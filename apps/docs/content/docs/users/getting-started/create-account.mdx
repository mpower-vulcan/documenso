## Accounts are created by invitation

SignCoach does not have open sign-up. You get an account one of two ways:

- **You are invited to an organisation.** An administrator adds you as a member and you receive an email
  with a link. Follow it, set a password, and you are in.
- **You are starting a new organisation.** Contact MPower Vulcan at
  [sign@mpowerglobal.ai](mailto:sign@mpowerglobal.ai) and an account and plan are set up for you.

<Callout type="info">
  People who only need to **sign** a document never need an account. They receive a link by email and sign
  from it.
</Callout>

## Set up your profile

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Sign in at [sign.mpowerglobal.ai](https://sign.mpowerglobal.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open the account menu (top right) and choose **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Set your full name as it should appear on documents, and add a signature. You can draw it, type it,
    or upload an image.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Under **Security**, turn on two-factor authentication or add a passkey.
  </Step>
</Steps>
